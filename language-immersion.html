Language and working culture

The language, and how the workplace uses it

Every immersion program works on two things together: the language itself, and the working culture you are entering. Each trainer brings years of teaching and professional experience.

How meetings run

Agendas, turn-taking, and follow-up: how to take the floor, raise a concern, and agree on next steps in the language of the room.

How decisions are made

Who is consulted and when, how a proposal is prepared, and the wording used to propose, agree, and disagree.

What register colleagues expect

Formal and informal address, the tone of email and chat, and how direct to be with managers, peers, and clients.

Optional component

Professional Transition Support

Language instruction and communication preparation for professionals joining a new company, entering a new role, or relocating to a different country. Programs combine intensive language development with preparation for the professional situations participants will meet in their first weeks.

This option adds communication preparation for the professional situations of your first weeks to the language lessons. It is agreed at the assessment and included in the same plan and package.

Who it is for

When the language cannot wait for a weekly lesson

Joining a new company or role

A new employer or a new position that works in a language you use less confidently: meetings, email, calls, and the first presentation to your new team.

Relocating to a new country

Working and living in Spain, Germany, or elsewhere: the language of the office and of everyday life, from day one.

Employers sending a colleague

Employers can sponsor the program directly and receive agreed progress updates, with the participant's knowledge and consent.

How it works

A plan built backwards from your first day

  1. 01Assessment and planYour current level, the role, the start date, and the situations that matter most in the first weeks.
  2. 02Daily lessonsCore language and the language of your work, practiced every day with materials taken from your field.
  3. 03Weekly reviewProgress against the plan, and adjustments as real situations come up at work.
  4. 04HandoverA clear next step: a regular Institute program, or support from Communication Consulting.

For professionals who need to develop their persuasive communication alongside their language skills, the Institute can connect the program with Making the Case, the Communication Consulting program in persuasive communication.

Practical questions

How many hours a day?
Agreed around your schedule and goals at the assessment, with time for practice between lessons.
Which level will I reach?
We set realistic goals at the start and review them every week. We do not promise a fixed level by a fixed date.
Can my employer pay?
Yes. Employers can sponsor the program directly and receive agreed progress updates, with the participant's knowledge and consent.
How is it priced?
As a package based on total hours and duration. We confirm the package after the first conversation.
Is this for beginners too?
Yes. Beginners are welcome, and the plan starts from your current level.
