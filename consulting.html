Audience and purpose first

We agree on the audience, language, setting, intended result, and required outputs before work begins. Every engagement is led or supervised by Dr. Dorian Kantor, with specialists involved where needed. Scope, timing, fees, and arrangements for sharing material are agreed at the outset.

How might this be understood?

A cross-cultural message review examines assumptions, wording, register, and context. It is an expert assessment of likely interpretation. Research with actual audience participants can be scoped separately when that is the right way to answer the question.

Track record

Dr. Dorian Kantor regularly delivers speeches in English and Spanish. He founded Kantor Consulting, a political analysis firm, and is a founding partner of Talveris Security and Defense, based in Latin America.

He has taught as a university professor and lecturer in Colombia (Pontificia Universidad Javeriana), Germany (Freie Universität Berlin), Czechia (University of Hradec Králové), and Hungary (Eötvös Loránd University).

His fields are international law, US foreign policy toward Latin America and Europe, and defense technology. He has done advisory work for the US and Colombian Armed Forces, ambassadors and judges in Colombia, and European political foundations and defense firms.
