Who we're looking for

Trainers who use the language at work

Every Kantor Communication trainer is hired at native or bilingual level and trained to apply the Kantor Method in their teaching.

Our learners are professionals: they need the language for meetings, negotiations, presentations, and research. We look for trainers who can work at that level.

  • Experienced, qualified language teachers and communication coaches.
  • Comfortable working with advanced (C1 and above), professional, executive, and academic learners.
  • Native or bilingual level in the language you teach.
  • A background in teaching, coaching, translation, or professional communication is a strong plus.
