Why Dorian Kantor

He makes the case for a living

Dr. Dorian Kantor has spent 15 years making complex ideas clear, compelling, and difficult to dismiss. He has advised the US and Colombian Armed Forces on security and defense, ambassadors on foreign policy, and judges on legal matters. He founded and led Kantor Consulting, a political analysis and consulting firm, and is a founding partner of Talveris Security and Defense. He has written for El Espectador, one of Colombia’s leading newspapers, and contributed expert commentary and analysis to international media outlets. His academic career spans universities in five countries. He brings that experience – and a sharp understanding of language, audiences, and persuasion – to every engagement.
