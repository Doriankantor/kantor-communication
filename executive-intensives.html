02

Each participant leaves with

  • Rehearsed answers to hard questions
  • Individual feedback

What participants practice

Substance, expression, practice

Participants work on their own upcoming situations and on contrasting cases, so they can name the principles behind a good response and carry them into the next negotiation. Every exercise ends with direct feedback.

  • SubstanceBuild an argument around interests and prepare a position
  • ExpressionFrame proposals, answer objections, and keep the right register in a second language
  • PracticeRepeat under pressure: questioning, conditional concessions, disagreement without loss of authority

How an intensive runs

From your situation to a rehearsed response

  1. BeforeSponsor briefing, a short interview with each participant, selection of real situations, a baseline exercise.
  2. Day 1Preparation, interests and positions, building the argument, questioning, first simulations.
  3. Day 2Objections, pressure, conditional concessions, difficult exchanges, repeated practice with feedback.
  4. Day 3 optionalComplex simulations, cross-cultural adaptation, team coordination, participants' own cases.
  5. AfterwardsTwo scheduled application sessions and a concise development plan for each participant.

What does an intensive cost?
The fee depends on duration, group size, and preparation. After a first conversation we propose a fixed fee covering preparation, delivery, and follow-up. Travel and venue are listed separately.
Where does it take place?
At your offices, or in Valencia, where we arrange the venue.
In which language?
English, German, or Spanish. Before booking, we confirm that every participant can work fully in the chosen language.
Can one executive book alone?
Yes. Individual preparation is commissioned through Communication Consulting, in a format adapted to one person: Does It Land? for a single decisive date, or Making the Case for a three-month program.
What happens to our real cases?
They stay confidential. We agree on how material is shared and handled before the intensive begins.
